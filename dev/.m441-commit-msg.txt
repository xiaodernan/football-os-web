M441: the app's chip is written once - the palette, and the colours the domain no longer names

A chip is the app's smallest coloured badge - a border, a wash and a word, all of one colour - and
the same chips were spelled out over and over: the load module's four zone chips, the news module's
three certainty chips, the review screen's three kind chips and the achievements screen's three tier
chips each wrote class strings that were, class for class, the same chip; the muted chip was written
out five times in four files; the chip an option wears when you have PICKED it was written out
sixteen times, in nine screens; and the offline notice's info chip was written out in three files.
So a caution could be one colour on the dashboard and another on the review page, and moving a chip
off its colour in one place left the others disagreeing about what it looks like.

The fold puts the chips in one table - ui/primitives.tsx's CHIP_CLASS: five tone chips, the two
muted chips, the option's two states and the filter chip's two states - and the load zone's map
(ZONE_CLASS) rides it. The colours leave the DOMAIN: domain/load.ts's ZONE_TONE and
domain/newsImpact.ts's CERTAINTY_TONE are gone, because a domain module that hands a screen a
Tailwind class string knows the app's colours and can disagree with the UI about them. The domain
still says what a zone means and how certain a read is; the UI says what it wears.

**The seam, charged both ways.** scripts/proof-chips.mjs is the census: it reads every quoted class
string in the app, keeps the ones that carry a border colour and a word colour, and charges three
shapes - the tone chip (a border, a wash and a word of one colour), the muted chip (the border
colour and the muted word) and the option at rest (muted words, and a border that appears when you
point at them). The rule is one sentence: a chip written twice lives in the palette, and a chip
written once is its screen's own. The reader is armed by planted chips - one of each shape, and two
shapes that are not chips - so the census is not empty and each shape is measured rather than
assumed; it also pins the two things the fold had to keep, the eleven classes the app wore and the
domain's freedom from colour (a planted domain module that carries a chip reads 1, so the zero
there is a measurement and not a blind spot). The file passes 8/8, and proof-zone-tone was rewritten
to the door's new home and keeps its old clauses - one declaration, three consumers that spread it,
the four classes every screen renders, and the pin that the /load banner's none still carries a wash
the other two do not - 8/8. The suite is green with tsc clean.

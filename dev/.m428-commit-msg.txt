M430: the colours for a tone - one mapping, and the screen that spelled it twice

M427 closed the app's colours for a DIFFERENCE. This unit closes the colours for a TONE - and
the app has two of those, because they are two different questions: a SIGN says which way a
figure moved, a TONE says how well a thing sits. The domain's judgement of a league position is
a tone - "good" | "mixed" | "bad" - and the app has three classes for it: green for good, blue
for mixed, red for bad. That mapping was spelled TWICE in features/manager/ManagerOS.tsx, with
the same three words in the same order: once for the season summary's honour and once for the
outlook's zone. A third, two-armed copy sat in the screen's own metric component.

So the app could have shown a mid-table club in blue on one card and in red on another, and a
change to the palette would have had to be made in two places.

The fold. TONE_CLASS - the three classes, keyed by the tone - lives in ui/primitives.tsx with
the rest of the shared UI vocabulary, and the manager screen reads it: the honour's tone (with
no honour at all reading as the middle blue, as it did), the zone's tone, and the metric
component's two arms (a metric with no tone stays uncoloured, as it did). Nothing a reader sees
moves: the proof replays the mapping against the inline forms it replaced, for all three tones.

The seam, charged both ways. New scripts/proof-toneclass.mjs (4/4) charges a COLOUR CHOSEN BY A
TONE - a comparison against one of the domain's tone words whose arms name a colour class - and
there are none outside the shared mapping. Two near shapes are refused, each for a reason: a
comparison against some OTHER string is not a tone, and the tile's own "up" | "down" | "neutral"
vocabulary is the TILE's, not the domain's - the tile's mapping lives inside the component,
which is where a component's contract belongs, and this census does not reach into it. The
reader is armed by a plant for each shape, the mapping is checked to be declared once, the
manager screen is checked to read it, and the three tones are replayed against the inline forms.

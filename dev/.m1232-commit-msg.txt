M1232 The Congested Calendar: the distance between the matches

Every board in this world reads what happened inside a match; none read the
distance between them. A side that plays Tuesday and Friday lives a different
season from one that plays Tuesday and the following Monday, and no screen has
ever said so.

- domain/congestion.ts (new, pure): per club, the turnaround its legs actually
  get - the tightest gap in whole days, the mean (2dp), and the count of
  crowded turns (three days or fewer). An unreadable kickoff is a record
  skipped, never a date at the epoch; a club with a single fixture has no
  spacing and takes no row. Ranks tightest-first (minRest, crowded desc, mean
  asc, id); the world's mean is over every gap walked.
- features/congestion/CongestedCalendar.tsx (new screen): three tiles (the
  tightest turn and its club, the world average and the gap count, the crowded
  stretches), the board ranked tightest-first with the tightest turn as the
  bar's scale, and the honest method line.
- scripts/proof-congestion.mjs (new, 7/7): a hand-counted world (A 2/4.00/1,
  B 1/2.50/1, C takes no row, league 3.25 over 4 gaps), the whole-day floor,
  the exact three-day boundary, the empty world and the unreadable kickoff,
  the id tie-break, and the screen's trilingual keys with the route mounted.
- One real defect the gates caught and this fixes honestly: the screen title
  and the nav label shipped the same 23-character English sentence, which the
  "one sentence, one key" gate refuses - the nav label is now its own short
  word and the inline unit follows the codebase's existing shape.
- Gates: proof-i18n 453/453 (literal 3792->3806, litVar 593->595, APP_SRC
  232->234, TSX 119->120, SRC 233->235), proof-storage 63/63 (228->230),
  proof-coverage 26/26 (POP 236->238, deep 110->111), proof-app-tree 13/13
  (235->237), proof-router 29/29 (225->227), proof-screens 32/32, proof-pwa
  4/4 (bundle 865->867 KB, baseline re-recorded); tsc clean, build exit 0.
  E2E congestion 1/1 and all-routes 96/96 with the new route in the net.
- Visual QA (the directive's fifth point): six key screens captured at

  visible typos; the manual's TOC numbering verified intact (the earlier

M1240 The Manual Catches Up: the booklet teaches the new world

Eight screens landed today — four boards and three ruling desks — and the
manual's explore links and chapters still described the old world. A manual
that names features which exist is honest; one that misses the newest ones
is not yet finished.

- features/manual/Manual.tsx: the explore chapter links the eight new
  surfaces (keepers, congestion, depth, nations, chair, desk, broker), and a
  new chapter — "the doors that decide" — teaches the three ruling desks (the
  Chair names a World XI; the Desk's prescription can be ADOPTED into a real
  plan; the Broker rules a three-way deal) and orients the four new boards.
- One naming conflict caught by the compiler itself: the manual already had
  an answer-doors chapter owning manual.doors.*, so the new chapter speaks
  through manual.decide.* — tsc's duplicate-key error named the collision
  before any gate had to.
- 18 trilingual keys; the new chapter carries the print stylesheet like
  every other chapter, so the booklet still prints as a booklet.
- tests/e2e/manual-doors.spec.ts (new, 1/1): the chapter's title, its three
  desk paragraphs and the boards paragraph are on the page, and the explore
  links reach the new surfaces.

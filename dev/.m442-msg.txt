M442: the census read a stretch of the file as a body, and the byte-identical twin behind it

M438 extended the rule census to local bodies and declared the layer closed.
This unit is the first family it could not see, found by fixing how it reads
a body. bodyOf decided a unit's shape by looking for a `{` on the body's
first line — but a concise arrow whose body carries an object literal
(`=> formatDate(..., { month: "short", ... })`) has a `{` there that is not
the function's brace, so the extractor brace-matched from inside the
statement and ran to a DISTANT close brace: the "body" was a stretch of the
file and the unit could never pair with its twin.

- The fix: the extraction match knows whether it consumed the function's `{`.
  A concise arrow scans to its own statement end (quote- and bracket-aware);
  a brace body keeps M417's conservative first-statement reading. With the
  bug fixed the census went RED immediately — Details.tsx:fmtDateTime ~
  FootballWorld.tsx:fmtDateTime, two byte-identical cross-file bodies, the
  pair the local clause exists to catch.
- fmtDateTime(locale, iso) door in i18n/format.ts (the date formatter's own
  home), re-exported from @/i18n beside formatDate; both pages' local copies
  deleted, they import the door. The door rides formatDate, so check 33's
  one-formatter rule still holds.

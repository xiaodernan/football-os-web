M424: the sign convention had a census, and the census charged one spelling of it

proof-signed is the gate for the app's signed-number door: a figure that can be
negative prints with a literal + when it is non-negative. Its census keys on ONE
spelling of that convention - ">= 0 ? "+" : """, a plus glued in front of a
pre-formatted number - and it reported, for the whole tree, that the numeric
sign ternary survives only in the door. The app had twelve more spellings, in a
shape that anchor cannot see: a plus glued to the number INSIDE a template, and
a comparison whose true arm is such a template.

The twelve: the Data Room's goal difference, the Compare page's edge, the Entity
Detail's category delta, the Scouting Panel's two edge deltas, the Watchlist
Board's two, the comparison's headroom metric, the potential headroom, the
session-growth toast, the XP tile and the transfer headroom.

And the two spellings disagreed. The door signs a NON-NEGATIVE figure ("+0"); the
residue signed only a POSITIVE one ("0"). So the same goal difference read "+0"
in the Manager OS season summary and "0" in the Data Room - one quantity in two
forms, on two screens a reader could compare, which is M401's class four hundred
commits later. M420's lesson, third sighting: a census charges the shapes its
author could think of.

All twelve now ride signed, the door whose docstring states the convention, and
the comparison page hands the door itself to its headroom metric rather than
wrapping it in a local formatter. A zero difference reads "+0" everywhere - the
word the app already gave a zero headroom and a zero XP gain - so the app now
agrees with itself. That is the one thing a reader sees move, and it is the
point.

- proof-signed gains three scans: a plus glued to a number in a template, a
  comparison whose true arm is such a template, and a private copy of the door's
  body. Each is armed by a plant, and the door and its padded sibling are shown
  not to be charged by them.
- The plus-template scan finds exactly one carrier, named in the proof: the
  Development page's gap badge, whose quantity is a SHORTFALL (target - actual)
  and whose sign is inverted on purpose - a room to develop reads "-3" and an
  exceeded target reads "+2". A different convention, not a residue.
- The residue's own shape survives nowhere; the door's body survives only in the
  door; all nine modules that used to spell the sign by hand import the door and
  use it (the scan counts a USE, not a call, because the comparison page passes
  the door as a value).
- lib/utils.ts: the door's docstring records the twelve and the one survivor.

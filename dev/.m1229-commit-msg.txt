M1229 The Teaching Hints: every button on the main interface now teaches

The master directive's fifth point named the gap by name: the main interface's
buttons and operations carry no teaching. This closes it.

- domain/teachHint.ts (new, pure): a 25-lesson catalogue (the dashboard's 22
  module tiles, the command palette, the manual and the operator console) and
  the seen-ledger arithmetic - markSeen is idempotent and refuses ids outside
  the catalogue, parseSeen degrades a corrupt store to a clean ledger, unseenIds
  is the complement in catalogue order.
- ui/TeachHint.tsx (new): the "?" bubble. Unread lessons pulse quietly, read
  ones stay out of the way; Escape or a tap outside closes; the aria-label
  announces an unread lesson as unread. The seen state lives under ONE storage
  key (football-os.teachSeen.v1), registered in localData as a preference so an
  activity reset keeps what you have read.
- Wiring: one lesson per dashboard module tile (the bubble sits OUTSIDE the
  anchor - reading a lesson never navigates), the palette trigger in the header,
  the manual's hero, the operator console, and a settings row that counts the
  unread lessons and marks them all unread again.

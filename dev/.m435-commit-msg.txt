M435: a vocabulary's list and its type are one declaration

M433's census read a UNION of string literals - but a vocabulary is also written as a LIST, and
the census could not see it. The scout found the drift in nine places:

- three screens spelled the app's three MODES out by hand - the command palette, the settings
  page and the app shell - while domain/types.ts held the list;
- the three LINE GROUPS (["ST","W","AM"], ["CM","DM"], ["CB","FB","GK"]) were written twice: in
  the domain's club ratings and in the club page's panel;
- the scout's four POSITION BUCKETS and the watch list's four ORDERINGS were each written twice as
  well;
- and in domain/types.ts itself, FIVE vocabularies spelled their words out twice - once as the
  type, once as the runtime list.

The fold. The type is now DERIVED from the list - the shape SEARCH_KINDS and FAVORITE_KINDS
already used - for twelve vocabularies, so the copy the compiler checks and the copy the runtime
walks are the same fact and cannot drift. The screens' copies now read the domain's list: the
modes, the line groups (LINE_GROUPS in domain/career.ts), the buckets (POS_BUCKETS) and the scout
sorts (SCOUT_SORTS).

The seam, charged both ways. proof-vocab now reads LISTS as well as unions: 46 vocabularies,
each spelled once, and 74 lists, each written once. Two lists are written twice on purpose and
are DECLARED as such: the search page's section order (players before clubs) and the matches
strip's button order (LIVE first) - the same words in the screen's own order, which is a
different decision from which words exist. The list reader is armed by plants for the two shapes
it refuses: a one-word array (a key, not a vocabulary) and a list of COLOURS (two clubs whose
kits share a shade are a coincidence, not a vocabulary). The file passes 6/6.

The i18n gate's anchors followed. The fold moved a vocabulary's definition, and the i18n gate
reads those definitions out of the source - on purpose, so a new tag fails until it has a key.
Four of its anchors read the unions this unit removed, and it went red with fourteen failures
before they were moved to the lists the types are derived from; the foot's clause, which compared
the union against the list, became a check that the type IS derived from the list - a stronger
claim than the one it replaced, because a hand-typed union is the one thing that could drift.

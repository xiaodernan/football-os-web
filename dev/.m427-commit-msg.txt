M427: the colours for a difference - one table, and the three files that spelled them

M426 closed the door that says WHICH WAY a difference points. This unit closes the one that says
what colour that is - and the app had three classes for it (green for a gain, red for a loss, grey
for a figure that did not move), spelled in three files:

- features/football/Details.tsx, for a CATEGORY difference;
- features/scouting/ScoutingPanel.tsx, for a player's BEST edge and WORST edge;
- features/watchlist/WatchlistBoard.tsx, for the SAME two edges, in the same words.

So the app could have shown the same figure in green on one screen and in grey on another, and a
change to the palette would have had to be made in three places. It is the third door in the
family M424 and M425 opened: the sign is the question, this is what the app does with the answer.

The fold. DELTA_CLASS - the three classes, keyed by the sign - and bestEdgeTone/worstEdgeTone
live in ui/primitives.tsx, beside the other shared UI vocabulary, and they ride M426's deltaSign
for the question. The two edge tones are deliberately two-armed: a best edge is green when it is
an advantage and grey when it is not, and it is never red - a red "best" would be a lie about the
best thing a player has going for them; a worst edge is red when it is a disadvantage and grey
when it is not, and never green. Nothing a reader sees moves: the proof replays both doors
against the inline forms they replaced, over ten figures including zero and negative zero.

The seam, charged both ways. New scripts/proof-deltacolor.mjs (4/4) charges a COLOUR CHOSEN BY
A COMPARISON - a comparison against zero whose arms name a colour class - and there are none
outside the shared vocabulary. Two near shapes are refused, each for a reason: a comparison
against a VARIABLE is some other decision (the manager's head-to-head, not a difference's sign),
and a comparison against a STRING is a different question again (the manager's "good" | "mixed" |
"bad" tone, which is a TONE and not a sign). The reader is armed by a plant for each shape, the
table and the two tones are checked to be declared once in the shared module, the three former
sites are checked to import them, and the doors are replayed against the inline forms.

100/100 proof scripts, 3048/3048 checks, tsc --noEmit clean.

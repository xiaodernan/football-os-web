M447: the label is written once too - the wash and the word, and the border and the wash

M446 closed the badge family and named what the census still could not see: a label
that carries a wash and a word with NO border (the community hub's "yours" label,
"bg-primary/15 text-primary") and its mirror, a border and a wash with no word (the
learning hub's right answer, "border-success bg-success/10"). This folds both into
the palette and teaches the reader to charge them.

The defect, measured before it was fixed. The primary wash on an icon plate
("bg-primary/15 text-primary") was written out in THIRTEEN places across eleven
screens - the AI studio, the community hub, the dashboard, the football world, the
learning hub, the manager OS, the player OS, the profile, the scouting panel, the
simulation lab and the tactical studio all wrote the same two classes by hand. The
"yours" chip wrote the same two classes a third time with a size beside them, and
the answer selection wrote its border-and-wash in two more. Twenty-one spellings of
one family, every one of them a copy of the rest, and the shade an icon plate wore
was decided by whichever screen had been edited last.

The fold. Fourteen entries joined the palette - ten labels and four marks - and the
twenty-one sites now name the palette. The class SET is unchanged: an entry carries
exactly the colour classes the site wore, and every other class the site wore (a
size, a padding, a ring) stays at the site, so nothing is painted differently. The
fold matched by COLOUR CLASS SET, not by substring: a string whose colours were
exactly an entry's set was folded, and one that carried an extra colour was left
alone - which is why the tone chip "border-primary bg-primary/15 text-primary" (a
border, a wash and a word) stayed a chip and did not become a label with a border
bolted on. The fold compared the class set before and after for every site and
refused to write a file whose set had changed.

The seam, charged both ways. The census now reads SIX shapes, and the last two are
the label and its mirror. The boundary is measured, not assumed, and it is measured
twice over. A LABEL is a wash and a word with no border; a MARK is a full-strength
border and a wash of that colour with no word. Two things are NOT labels and the
reader says so: a wash of a SURFACE colour (a card, a popover, a sidebar) is a
panel, and a SOFTENED border ("border-success/40") is a container's edge, not a
mark - a border at full strength is the colour itself, a border with an opacity
suffix is a hint of one. The reader is planted with both wrong readings alongside
the right ones, so the zeros below are a measurement and not a blind spot, and the
two new clauses are planted with a screen that spells a label and a mark out, so
the clauses are seen REFUSING rather than only reported clean. The palette now
holds thirty-one entries (eleven chips, six badges, ten labels and four marks) and
the reader checks all thirty-one against the text the app wore before the fold, and
twenty-four screens are checked for reading it. proof-chips 12/12; proof-record 5/5
(239 headings); proof-ledger-run 5/5; tsc --noEmit clean.

M425: a number that decides is written in one place - the threshold census, and four decisions it found

M418 closed the quantity class (a field formatted two ways) and M424 the sign class (a
convention spelled thirteen ways). This unit is the third of the family: a THRESHOLD - a
number the app compares a value against to decide something. A threshold is a decision,
and a decision written twice is a decision the two copies can disagree about.

What the scout found, by hand, in one pass over the tree:

- The league position band - the top quarter and the upper middle of a table, over the
  club's share of the field - was written twice in domain/career.ts, three hundred lines
  apart: the outlook's zone and the season summary's honour each spelled the same two
  bounds (0.25 and 0.6) over the same share.
- The exhausted line - the fatigue above which a squad counts as spent - was written
  twice: the match prep's "play slow" and the Manager OS's fatigue tile, in a domain
  module and a screen. A change to the line would have moved the app's BEHAVIOUR without
  moving the tile that reports it.
- The level bands - the three bands of a 0..1 level, 0.35 and 0.7 - were written four
  times: the Manager OS's tempo and press words and the Simulation Lab's two slider
  captions. The Manager OS's own comment said the bands were "the lab's bands", and the
  code next to it spelled them again.
- The Data Room re-derived the fixture forecast the data layer already owns - its own
  metrics map, its own "too little form data" guard, its own odds and percentages - while
  the Football World beside it called the domain's fixtureForecast door.
- Two local copies: the review's four-stars line, written twice in one block, and the
  Manager OS's coin-flip line, written twice in one card.

The fold. positionBand(position, clubCount) in domain/attributes.ts owns the three
bands; fatigueIsHigh(fatigue) in domain/career.ts owns the line; tempoLevel/pressLevel in
domain/rowText.ts own the level bands AND the two vocabularies - a tempo is "low" and a
press is "deep", so the band is one decision and the word is the quantity's. The Data
Room now asks the data layer for its forecast, like the Football World already did, and
the two local copies are read once each. Nothing a reader sees moves: every door answers
exactly what the inline form answered, over every sample the proof replays.

The seam, charged both ways. New scripts/proof-thresholds.mjs (7/7) reads every
comparison of a value against a NUMERIC LITERAL above one - zero and one are presence,
not magnitude - and skips two more shapes with a reason each: a loop's header is a count
of repetitions, and a comparison against a random draw is a probability, not a bound.
What is left is grouped by the number it compares against: 93 thresholds over 64
numbers, 13 of them compared in more than one place. Every such group must be
DECLARED - as a decision that lives in a door (exactly one site, in that door's home) or
as a split (different decisions that share a number, with the site count, the file count
and the reason pinned). The reader is armed by plants for each exclusion, the door
groups are checked against their own homes, the splits against their pinned shapes, and
the doors are replayed against the inline forms they replaced.

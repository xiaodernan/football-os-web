M426: the sign of a difference - one question, four copies, and the words each copy chose

M424 closed the door that prints a figure WITH ITS SIGN. This unit closes the door that says
WHICH WAY THE FIGURE POINTS - the three-arm ladder: compare the difference with zero, and with
zero the other way round, and call the result up, down or flat. The app asked that question in
four places, and each copy answered in its own vocabulary:

- domain/compare.ts, for an ATTRIBUTE edge: delta > 0 ? "a" : delta < 0 ? "b" : "even";
- domain/compare.ts again, for a CATEGORY edge: the same ladder, in the same file;
- features/football/Details.tsx, for a category difference's COLOUR;
- features/review/ReviewPage.tsx, for the change in a player's OVERALL.

So a change to what counts as "no difference" would have moved one screen and left the other
three reading the same figure differently. It is the same shape of defect as M424's, one door
over: a question the app asks, asked again.

The fold. deltaSign(d) in domain/attributes.ts answers it once and returns "up" | "down" |
"flat". The WORDS stay where they belong - each reader keeps its own table, keyed by the sign
so no arm can be swapped by accident: FAVOR_OF in the comparison page, DELTA_TONE in the
entity detail, DELTA_TILE in the Weekly Review. The question is the domain's; the vocabulary
is the screen's. Nothing a reader sees moves: the door answers exactly what the ladder
answered on every figure a reader could meet, zero and negative zero included.

The seam, charged both ways. New scripts/proof-deltasign.mjs (4/4) charges a THREE-ARM SIGN
LADDER - one expression that asks "is this above zero?" and then "is this below zero?" - and
it survives in exactly one place: the door. Two near shapes are refused, each because it is a
different contract: a TWO-ARMED SIGN asks whether a figure is positive, not which way it
points, and the app has five of those for a different reason; an EVEN BAND over a tolerance is
a third decision again - the comparison page's own verdict, where a small advantage is a draw.
The reader is armed by a plant for each of the three, the door is checked to be declared once,
and every module that used to spell the ladder is checked to ask it.

99/99 proof scripts, 3044/3044 checks, tsc --noEmit clean.

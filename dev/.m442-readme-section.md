### M442 - the badge is written once too: a border and a word, with no wash of its own

**The defect.** M441 folded the app's **chip** - a border, a wash and a word, all of one colour - into
one table, and named the family it did not charge: the **badge**, which is a border and a word of
one colour with **no wash of its own**. The app spelled those out too:

- the **amber badge** (`border-warning/40 text-warning`) was written out in **seven** places - the
  community hub's three size-10 badges, the film room's confidence badge, the detail page's two
  badges and the player screen's demo-confidence badge;
- the **green badge** (`border-success/40 text-success`) was written out in **five** - the detail
  page's two, the learning hub's, the profile page's inactive goal and the dynamic world's;
- the **stronger amber badge** (`border-warning/50 text-warning`) was written out twice - the
  dashboard's and the transfer centre's neutral stance;
- and three more, once each.

**The fold.** The badges join the palette beside the chips (`CHIP_CLASS.badgeSuccess`,
`badgeWarning`, `badgePrimary`, `badgeWarningStrong`, `badgeSuccessStrong`, `badgeInfoStrong`), and
the sixteen sites read them.

**The seam, charged both ways.** The census grows a **fourth shape**: a badge is a border and a word
of one colour with no wash, and the rule the shape joins is the same one - a badge written twice
lives in the palette, and a badge written once is its screen's own. The reader is armed by a planted
badge, so the new shape is measured and not assumed.

**Still outstanding.** The census still charges only the shapes its author could name, and there is
one more shape the app wears that none of the four covers: a **plain coloured word** with no border
and no wash (`text-success` on its own), which the app uses for the difference between two numbers
and a few other marks. That family is the next one to name.

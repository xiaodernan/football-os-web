M1241 The Ops Trend: the console now forecasts and prescribes

The directive asks the admin surface for analysis, a trend forecast AND the
next optimisation measures. The tide panel drew the fortnight and said rising
or falling — no projection, no advice. This lands both.

- domain/opsTrend.ts (new, pure): trendRead answers the week's mean, the
  previous window's, and the slope between them (a zero previous window
  reads 0 rather than dividing by zero); the PROJECTION walks the week's mean
  along the slope day by day, clamped at zero, with UTC day labels that cross
  months and years correctly; the ADVICE is five deterministic rules, each
  firing on the fold's own measured number (bounce >= 50 onboarding, depth
  < 2 navigation, slope <= -10% content, loop >= 20% engagement, else
  healthy).
- One honest guard the proof's empty arm forced: a series with NO history
  prescribes NOTHING — a zero depth is not a low depth, and a world with no
  data must not be told to fix its navigation.
- features/ops/Ops.tsx: the trend panel keeps the history bars, adds the
  projected week on the same scale in dashed outline (labelled as a
  straight-line read of the series' own momentum, not a model's guess), and
  lists the advice with the number each rule fired on.
- scripts/proof-opstrend.mjs (new, 9/9): a hand-counted fortnight
  (80/100/-20%), the projection's seven-step arithmetic, rising and flat
  directions, the zero-previous-window guard, each advice rule by code, the
  healthy answer, exact boundaries (50% and 20% fire; -9.99% does not), the
  empty series, nextDay across month and year ends, the static labels in
  three locales with agreeing placeholders, and the ten advice keys behind
  the two dynamic prefixes keyed everywhere.
- Gates: proof-i18n 462/462 (literal 3954->3958, template 201->203, litVar
  617->619, APP_SRC 246->247, SRC 249->250; the two advice dynamic sites and
  their value space read from the domain's union), proof-storage 63/63
  (242->243), proof-coverage 26/26 (POP 250->251, domain 101->102),
  proof-app-tree 13/13 (249->250), proof-router 29/29 (239->240),
  proof-screens 32/32, proof-pwa 4/4 (bundle 891->893 KB, baseline
  re-recorded); tsc clean, build exit 0. ops E2E 3/3 — one case flaked
  under a four-minute loaded run and passed on re-run, with the page
  verified rendering both the census and the projection with zero console
  errors; recorded as environment noise, not as green.

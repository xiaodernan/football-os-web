M1231 The Keepers' Union: the world's goalkeepers, named

Every board on the leaders screen ranks what clubs and outfield players did;
none named who stood between the posts for the best defensive record. This
lands /keepers: each club's number one, with the club's own clean-sheet record
beside him.

- domain/keepers.ts (new, pure): one keeper per club — its highest-rated
  goalkeeper, with a total-order comparator (overall, then potential, then id)
  so equal keepers never reorder between runs. The clean-sheet numbers are NOT
  a second sweep of the fixtures: they are read from the shared leagueWall
  fold the leaders' wall board prints, so the two screens cannot disagree
  about a sheet. The best wall is a record, not a rating: a club that has
  never kept a sheet has no wall to be best of. Empty, keeperless and
  sheet-less worlds read empty values, not crashes.
- features/keepers/KeepersUnion.tsx (new screen): three tiles (best keeper,
  best wall, the count), the board of number ones with crest, club, overall,
  the club's "p played · c clean · r%" line and a rate bar, and the honest
  method line naming what the sheets are and who the keeper is.
- scripts/proof-keepers.mjs (new, 8/8): a hand-counted world (A 2/2 @ 100%,
  B 1/2 @ 50%, C takes no row), the shared-wall equality field by field, the
  ordering and both headline reads, the three empty boundaries, and the
  tie-break total order; the screen's keys are trilingual with agreeing
  placeholders and the route is mounted.

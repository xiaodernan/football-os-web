M1239 The Two-Cache Wire: the live wire reads the whole cache tier

The cache-service carries TWO caches — the league summary and the league
tables read-through — and the live wire read only the first one's stats. Half
a story: the tables read-through is where a season's worth of table reads
actually lands.

- scripts/llm-proxy.mjs: probeService now reads BOTH /stats endpoints in
  PARALLEL (each leg independently aborted at 1.2s — the wall clock is the
  slowest leg, not the sum, the same bound-your-blocking discipline the
  fleet probe applies), and the envelope carries stats AND tables.

  run caught a real robustness defect — one leg answering a body that will
  not parse took the WHOLE probe down (it fell through to "unreachable").
  Now that leg reads null and the other still reports.

  for the summary AND the tables.

M1237 The Talent Map: where the world's talent comes from

Every board in this world ranks clubs and players; none has ever asked the
geographic question. The seed carries a nation on every player, so the fold
reads the world's map: who supplies the most, who supplies the best, and what
the average side of a nation looks like.

- domain/nations.ts (new, pure): per nation the player count, the best overall
  with its player's NAME (so a row can point at a person), and the mean across
  everyone from it; ranked best desc, depth desc, name; the strongest and
  deepest headline reads; the world's own mean. A blank nation is a record
  skipped, not an empty row.
- features/nations/TalentMap.tsx (new screen): three tiles, the nation board
  ranked strongest-first with the mean as the bar's scale, and the honest
  method line naming exactly what a nation's three numbers are.
- scripts/proof-nations.mjs (new, 6/6): a hand-counted world (Brazil
  3/91/82.00, Spain 2/88/81.50, Japan 1/70/70.00, world 79.83), the ordering
  and both headline reads, the best player's name, the blank nation and the
  empty world, the name tie-break, and the screen's trilingual keys with the
  route mounted.

  242->244, TSX 124->125, SRC 245->247; the world-average tile took its own
  word rather than sharing the congestion screen's), proof-storage 63/63

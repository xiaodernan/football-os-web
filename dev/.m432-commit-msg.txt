M433: a set of words that means one thing is spelled in one place - the vocabulary census

M427 and M428 closed the app's two colour vocabularies, and M430 said what remained: the
remaining risk is a fourth vocabulary appearing with no door behind it, and that is the class a
census of VOCABULARIES would charge. This unit writes that census - and it found nine
vocabularies spelled more than once, FOUR of them in a file that already had a name for them:

- the career's TONE, "good" | "mixed" | "bad" - five times: the zone table, the honour table,
  the two interfaces that carry them, and the interface the app's colours are keyed by;
- the comparison page's FAVOUR, "a" | "b" | "even" - four times in one file;
- the FOOT, "L" | "R" - the type was named in domain/types.ts, and the seed spelled it out twice
  anyway;
- the FORM LETTER, "W" | "D" | "L" - named in analytics, spelled out twice in attributes;
- the SIDE, "home" | "away" - in the engine, in the types and in the seed;
- the TILE's direction, "up" | "down" | "neutral" - the tile's prop, the review's table and the
  load page's own;
- the engine's EVENT SIDE, "H" | "A" - twice in the engine;
- the MODE, "FAN" | "PLAYER" | "MANAGER" - named in the types, spelled out in the shell;
- the MATCH STATUS, "SCHEDULED" | "LIVE" | "FINISHED" - named in the types, spelled out in the
  world screen.

A vocabulary spelled twice is a vocabulary that can drift: a word added to one copy leaves the
other copy out of it, and the compiler cannot see it, because a union of literals is a type, not
a value.

The fold. Each of the nine is a NAMED TYPE now, declared once and exported: Tone, Side, Foot,
Mode and MatchStatus in domain/types.ts (the app's type home - the words live with the types,
and a type import costs no bundle), FormLetter in domain/attributes.ts (where the ladder and the
letter door already live, re-exported by analytics for the callers that already import its
types), Favor in domain/compare.ts, EventSide in domain/matchEngine.ts and TileTone in
ui/primitives.tsx (the tile's own vocabulary, named so the screens that hand a tile a tone write
the three words once).

The seam, charged both ways. New scripts/proof-vocab.mjs (4/4) reads every UNION OF STRING
LITERALS in the app and requires it to be spelled in exactly one place: 59 vocabularies, 59
spellings, none twice. Comments are prose about the code, not code, so the reader strips them
first - the dictionary's own note about the foot's two words is not a second spelling - and it
is armed by a plant for each shape it refuses. The nine vocabularies are then checked to be
named types, each declared once and exported, and the thirteen places that used to spell the
words out are checked to name them instead.
